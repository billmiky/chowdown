ingredients:
- 1 tablespoon extra-virgin olive oil, plus more for greasing
- 1 pound ground turkey (at least 6-percent fat)
- ½ cup (4 ounces) whole-milk cottage cheese
- ⅓ cup chopped basil, plus 1 basil sprig (optional)
- ⅓ cup finely grated Parmesan
- 1 garlic clove, finely grated
- Kosher salt (such as Diamond Crystal)
- 1 (24-ounce) jar marinara sauce
directions:
- Arrange an oven rack near the broiler and set the broiler to high. Add the olive oil to a large, broiler-safe skillet and
  swirl it around. In a medium bowl, mix together the turkey, cottage cheese, chopped basil (if using), Parmesan, garlic and
  ½ teaspoon salt.
- Using oiled hands, roll the meat mixture into 12 balls (about 3 tablespoons/ 1½ ounces each) and place in the skillet as
  you go. If the meat is not holding its shape, refrigerate for 5 to 10 minutes to firm before rolling. Broil until browned
  on top (but not necessarily cooked through), 5 to 8 minutes.
- Transfer the skillet to the stovetop. Carefully pour in the marinara sauce and add the basil sprig (if using). Set over
  medium-high. Once simmering, reduce heat to medium and simmer, stirring gently and occasionally, until the meatballs are
  cooked through, 5 to 8 minutes.
