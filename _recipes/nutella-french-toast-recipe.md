ingredients:
- 2 large eggs
- 1/2 cup milk
- 2 tablespoons sugar
- 1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1/3 cup Nutella
- 4 slices white bread
directions:
- In a dish wide enough to fit a slice of the bread, mix together the eggs, milk, sugar, and vanilla extract with a fork or
  whisk.
- Spread a generous layer of Nutella on each slice of bread, then put the slices together to make 2 sandwiches.
- Heat some butter or neutral oil in a large frying pan over medium heat.
- Dip a sandwich into the egg mixture, covering both sides thoroughly.
- Fry for about 2 minutes on each side until browned. Repeat with the second sandwich.
- Slice into halves, and serve with a dusting of powdered sugar and some fresh strawberries.
